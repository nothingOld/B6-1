Q11
유형: 집계 - COUNT + GROUP BY
목적: 회원별 리뷰 작성 횟수를 조회한다.

[SQL]
SELECT m.member_id
     , m.name
     , COUNT(r.review_id) AS review_count
  FROM member AS m
  LEFT JOIN review AS r
    ON m.member_id = r.member_id
 GROUP BY m.member_id
        , m.name
 ORDER BY review_count DESC;
