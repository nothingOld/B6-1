Q10
유형: 집계 - AVG + GROUP BY
목적: 리뷰가 존재하는 영화의 평균 평점을 조회한다.

[SQL]
SELECT mo.movie_id
     , mo.title
     , AVG(r.rating) AS average_rating
  FROM movie AS mo
 INNER JOIN review AS r
    ON mo.movie_id = r.movie_id
 GROUP BY mo.movie_id
        , mo.title
 ORDER BY average_rating DESC;
