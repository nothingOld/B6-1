Q09
유형: 집계 - COUNT + GROUP BY
목적: 영화별 리뷰 개수를 조회한다.

[SQL]
SELECT mo.movie_id
     , mo.title
     , COUNT(r.review_id) AS review_count
  FROM movie AS mo
  LEFT JOIN review AS r
    ON mo.movie_id = r.movie_id
 GROUP BY mo.movie_id
        , mo.title
 ORDER BY review_count DESC;
